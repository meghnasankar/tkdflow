      The three rule profiles

      • World Taekwondo, in force from 1 November 2026. Points: 1 for a valid punch to the trunk protector, 2 for a trunk kick, 3 for a head kick; a valid turning kick doubles them to 4 or 6. The PSS sensors decide whether a kick lands with enough impact; judges validate punches and add the turning points; the centre referee declares Gam-jeom penalties, each worth 1 point to the opponent. Only crossing the boundary line, falling down and running away or avoiding (and a rejected technical review) give 2 points in a round’s last 10 seconds. WT Competition Rules and Interpretation (PDF) · WT rules directory
      • AAU Taekwondo youth safety rules, effective 1 November 2026 (Olympic-style sparring, all belts aged 5–11 and colour belts aged 12–17): head contact must be light and controlled; a light head kick scores 3, and a turning kick to the head gets no extra points. AAU Taekwondo Rules Handbook (Article 22)
      • AAU Taekwondo Grassroot divisions, effective 1 November 2026: no head contact at any age or rank. A kick that touches the head is a misconduct penalty against the kicker; a head kick that misses may get a verbal warning. AAU Taekwondo Rules Handbook (Article 23) · AAU Taekwondo

      Training as a referee

      Referees qualify through their organization’s own courses, seminars and supervised events. These scenarios can help you practise reading exchanges, but they don’t count toward any qualification. The referee guide covers the rules behind them.

      • USA Taekwondo University: kyorugi referee courses: the course path from foundations to centre-referee procedures, alongside events, camps and seminars.
      • AAU Taekwondo: current rules, clinics and officials’ registration.
      • World Taekwondo rules and regulations: the current competition rules.
