## Account age policy — first stage (2026-11-15, local, not live)

The owner approved the product age rules: public lessons for all ages; individual accounts 13+; 13–17 private by default with restricted sharing; under 13 only through a future parent/guardian-managed private child profile with no independent child login. This is a **policy decision**, not permission to reopen accounts and not a compliance claim. Full record and remaining gates: [account-age-policy.md](account-age-policy.md).

- **Prepared (tested locally; accounts still off, so not reachable on the site):**
  - A neutral age screen comes before any account details and before Google can start. It uses free entry with no default and no threshold hint, and the age is never stored.
  - A per-tab `sessionStorage` flag keeps only `eligible` or `ineligible`. Once ineligible, reloading or pressing back gives no second try.
  - Under-13s get a public-lessons and parent-guidance route with no account form; hosting and font requests still occur.
  - Flow gates mean that without an explicit `eligible` result there is no `signUp`, username RPC, school write, profile write or OAuth. A typed age under 13 stops the flow before any call.
  - Password sign-in, reset and logout, and routing for existing members, are unchanged for visitors who are unscreened or eligible.
  - A tab with a known under-13 result keeps the public guidance for every auth mode (login, forgot, recovery, complete, register) and on the profile page, before any form appears or the account client starts. This fixes ROOT's local browser finding that the header "Log in" link still showed the login and reset forms after an age of 12.
  - A legacy profile whose stored age is under 13 is held: it isn't shown, edited or written, local sign-out is attempted, and nothing is deleted or converted. This doesn't block direct API access.
  - The eligibility store reads `sessionStorage` inside `try`, so a browser that throws from the getter falls back to memory.
  - Profile age is 13–99 in the UI, JS and SQL (`profiles_age_individual`, NOT VALID). The upgrade from the 162a7aa hardened schema and re-runs are tested; legacy under-13 rows are kept but cannot be saved again.
  - Preflight now counts under-13 and unknown-age rows and shows which age rule is installed.
  - Planned-rules copy is on `about.html#age-policy`, labelled as a plan and not a privacy policy. Public pages are described as usable "without entering account details", not "no personal details", because Google Fonts requests and hosting logs still apply.
- **Tests:** `age-gate.test.mjs` (boundaries 12/13 and 17/18, malformed or unknown answers, sticky flag, blocked storage, neutral markup, accounts-off guard), `auth-flows.test.mjs` (no calls before eligibility, Google and completion gates, unchanged member routes), `profile-model.test.mjs` and `db.test.mjs` (under-13 rejected, hardened-schema upgrade and re-run, VALIDATE only after review, `auth.users` not gated by SQL).
- **Independent verification:** full local suite 283/283 passes; after the final pending-response guard, 100/100 relevant account, age, syntax and site checks pass. Chrome with an isolated fake-account service verified blank-age handling, age 12 public guidance, reload and header-login protection, age 13 registration, Google gating, direct recovery/forgot/completion routes with zero visible fields, and the legacy-child profile hold. These are simulated flows, not real Supabase staging tests. The shipped accounts-off auth and profile pages each showed zero visible inputs and no SDK script element. The new policy heading now clears the fixed header (90 px versus 69 px) after following its link. New work remains local and uncommitted; PR #2 still ends at 162a7aa.
- **Still blocking:**
  - server-side enforcement when an account is created (SQL profile rules do not gate `auth.users`);
  - privacy notice, retention and deletion processes;
  - audience and third-party review;
  - a delivery test of `support@tkdflow.com` (configured, not yet tested);
  - parent-managed child profiles (consent, relationship, permissions, review and delete), which are not built;
  - staging and owner approval.
