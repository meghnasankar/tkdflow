For parents and guardians

A child profile managed by a parent or guardian is planned but not available yet. It will open only after verified parental consent, server-side permissions, a deletion and retention process, and a legal and audience review are in place. Until then, please practise together using the public pages, and learn alongside a qualified instructor.
