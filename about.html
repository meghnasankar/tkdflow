About TKDflow & project status

TKDflow is a free taekwondo forms catalogue and training resource. This page explains what works today, what is paused, and what has not been decided yet.

What works today

  • Forms library — a searchable catalogue of WT/Kukkiwon, ITF and ATA forms.
  • Taegeuk Il Jang lesson — an animated, step-through lesson.

Paused or in preview

  • Member accounts are paused. The previous account service could not be reached, and accounts will only reopen after the privacy, audience and data-retention arrangements below are decided.
  • Community and Forum are previews with example content only; nothing can be posted.
  • Events shows an archive of older, unverified listings. New listings will only be added with a link to the organiser’s official page.

How training content is reviewed

Lesson and catalogue content has not yet been reviewed by a qualified instructor. Movement counts, belt levels and descriptions may contain mistakes, and belt mappings vary between schools and organisations. Each lesson will record its reference source, reviewer and review date once it has been reviewed. Until then, please learn alongside your own instructor.

Privacy & your data

A privacy policy has not been published yet. Because member accounts are paused, the site currently has no working sign-up or profile forms. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the hosting provider may keep standard request logs.

Before accounts reopen, TKDflow will publish a privacy policy that covers what is collected, who can see it, how long it is kept, how to correct or delete it, and how younger members are protected.

Contact

A public contact address has not been set up yet. It will be listed here once it exists.

Credits & licensing

Licensing terms for the site’s code and content have not been published yet. Form names and descriptions refer to the curricula of World Taekwondo / Kukkiwon, the International Taekwon-Do Federation and the American Taekwondo Association. Any affiliation or endorsement will be stated here only once it has been confirmed.
