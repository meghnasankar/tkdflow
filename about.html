About

About TKDflow & project status

TKDflow is a free taekwondo forms catalogue and training resource. Created by Meghna Sankar to make taekwondo education clearer and easier to explore.

What works today

  • Forms library — a searchable catalogue of WT/Kukkiwon, ITF and ATA forms.
  • 3D form lessons — source-based, step-through animations across the catalogue.
  • Community resources and discussion guides — practical questions about forms, training and competitions.
  • Worldwide events — official 2026–2027 listings, filters and major championships.

Member accounts

Sign-in is temporarily unavailable while TKDflow reconnects its account service. Learning resources and discussion guides are open to everyone without an account.

Who is behind TKDflow

TKDflow was founded by Meghna Sankar, a second-degree Kukkiwon black belt who has trained in taekwondo since she was five. Meet Meghna.

Created from experience and research

TKDflow’s learning materials are created by Meghna Sankar, a second-degree Kukkiwon black belt, drawing on her training experience, research and published taekwondo references. The guides help students explore forms and understand how curricula vary between schools.

Privacy & your data

Everyone can use the public lessons without creating an account or entering account details. Pages still load Google Fonts and the host may keep standard request logs.

Account ages and privacy

When sign-in is restored, individual accounts are for people aged 13 and over. Accounts aged 13 to 17 remain private, with no public posting or messaging. A parent or guardian aged 18 or over would manage any future child profile; child profiles do not exist yet. Account creation is currently unavailable. These participation rules are not a privacy policy.

A privacy policy has not been published yet. While the account service is unavailable, the site currently has no working sign-up or profile forms. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the hosting provider may keep standard request logs.

Before accounts reopen, TKDflow will publish a privacy policy that covers what is collected, who can see it, how long it is kept, how to correct or delete it, and how younger members are protected.
