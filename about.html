About

About TKDflow & project status

TKDflow is a free taekwondo forms catalogue and training resource. Created by Meghna Sankar to make taekwondo education clearer and easier to explore.

What works today

  • Forms library — a searchable catalogue of WT/Kukkiwon, ITF and ATA forms.
  • 3D form lessons — source-based, step-through animations across the catalogue.
  • Community discussions and learning resources — practical questions about forms, training and competitions.
  • Worldwide events — official 2026–2027 listings, filters and major championships.

Member accounts

Individual accounts are open to ages 13 and over. Create and confirm an account by email, then connect Google if you want to use it for sign-in. You can choose your school from the directory or submit a missing school for review. Learning resources and discussion guides remain available without an account.

Who is behind TKDflow

TKDflow was founded by Meghna Sankar, a second-degree Kukkiwon black belt who has trained in taekwondo since she was five. Meet Meghna.

Created from experience and research

TKDflow’s learning materials are created by Meghna Sankar, a second-degree Kukkiwon black belt, drawing on her training experience, research and published taekwondo references. The guides help students explore forms and understand how curricula vary between schools.

Privacy & your data

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

TKDflow is a learning project created by Meghna Sankar. This notice explains how account information, private study saves and discussions are handled.

Learning without an account

Everyone can use the public lessons without creating an account or entering account details. Fonts, animations, audio and site scripts load from TKDflow. No advertising or analytics tracker is installed by TKDflow. The hosting provider still receives normal connection information, such as an IP address, browser information and the requested page, to deliver and protect the site.

Age and account rules

  • Individual accounts are for people aged 13 and over. The age screen appears before account details. Its exact answer is checked and discarded; a private teen or adult band is kept to apply the account rules.
  • Members aged 13 to 17 have private profiles and cannot publish posts or messages.
  • Adults aged 18 or over with completed profiles can submit discussions for moderation. Their profiles remain private.
  • Children under 13 can use the public learning resources without an account. Parent-managed child profiles do not exist yet; there is no child account or parent-consent service.

Information you choose to provide

Email registration uses an email address and password. A profile requires a name, username and belt level. Profile age and school are optional; if you provide them, they are saved with your profile. Passwords are handled by Supabase, the account service.

Your profile details are not shown to other members. Site administrators can access account information when needed for support and administration. Your school membership stays private. If you add a school that is not listed, its public business details are saved in a private admin review queue after your confirmed account has a profile. Approved school details join the shared directory under the Open Database License; your identity and membership are never included in that listing. Existing unlisted school names can also be queued for review.

Approved adult discussion posts and replies are public, including their text, username and posting date. They do not show the account email, full name, age or private profile. Pending submissions are available to their author and moderators; reports are available to moderators. Private messaging is not available.

Saved forms and lesson progress

If you use the save or study controls without signing in, TKDflow keeps form IDs, the last studied animation pose and optional practised marks in this browser. These browser-only saves can be seen by anyone using the same browser profile. They are not linked to an account or automatically uploaded when you sign in.

For signed-in members with a confirmed email and completed profile, saved forms, study positions, practised marks and their update times are stored privately with the account so they can be used across devices. Other members and forum moderators cannot read them. You can remove a saved form or clear its progress in My learning; deleting the account removes its study records. You can choose to copy browser-only saves into your account from My learning. No public leaderboard or activity feed is created.

Lesson questions

Signed-in members aged 13 and over with a confirmed email and completed profile, including members aged 13 to 17, can send a private question about a lesson. The question text, the lesson, the animation pose it refers to, its review status and the time it was sent are stored with the account. Only the member and TKDflow community moderators can read the question and the moderators’ replies; moderators see a private member label, not the member’s email, school, profile or study history. Questions are not emailed and are never published automatically.

The public lesson answers page shows questions and answers written separately by moderators for everyone. It never includes a member’s private question text or name. Answers show the moderator credit chosen for that answer. Deleting an account removes its lesson questions and their replies; you can also ask for a copy, correction or deletion through the support address below.

Optional Google sign-in

Create and confirm an account by email first. You can then connect Google for sign-in. With your permission, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the account service. These details are used for sign-in and account setup. TKDflow does not request access to Gmail, Drive or Calendar, and does not receive your Google password.

Services and browser storage

Vercel hosts the site. Supabase handles accounts and stores profile, private study, lesson question and discussion data. Resend sends confirmation and password-reset emails. Google handles optional Google sign-in and the support inbox. Namecheap forwards messages sent to the public support address. These services process the connection, account or delivery information needed for their role.

Your browser stores the sign-in session so you can stay signed in, and a tab-only eligibility result for the age screen. Signing out removes that device's local sign-in session; it does not delete the account.

Retention, corrections and deletion

Account and profile information is kept to operate your account while it exists. There is no automatic inactive-account deletion schedule. Service providers also keep operational, security or delivery logs under their service terms.

You can edit your private profile, or request a copy, correction or deletion of account information through the support address below. Requests are handled individually after account ownership is checked. Approved discussions can remain public after an account is removed; include the links to any posts or replies you want removed in your request.

Please send an account request from its registered email address. Never send a password, confirmation link or reset link to support. Changes to this notice will be reflected in the date above.

Contact

Email support@tkdflow.com for content questions, account help, privacy requests or concerns about a discussion.
