Founder

Meghna Sankar Founder of TKDflow

Meghna Sankar is a second-degree Kukkiwon black belt who has trained in taekwondo since she was five. She founded TKDflow to make forms, school curricula, belt levels and competition information easier for students to understand.

  • Rank 2nd-degree black belt, Kukkiwon
  • Training Since age five
  • Competition State and national titles
  • Service U.S. Naval Sea Cadet

The purpose

Why she created TKDflow

Schools can follow different curricula and learning paths. Students may need help understanding their own forms and belt levels while exploring how other systems work.

Meghna’s aim is to make information about forms, curricula, belt levels and competitions clearer and easier to find. Her longer-term vision includes an open discussion board where students and taekwondo enthusiasts can exchange questions and ideas.

Explore the forms library and Community for discussions and learning resources. Adult members can submit questions and replies for moderator review.

TKDflow

What she is building

TKDflow brings together the things a student needs between classes: a way to find the forms their school teaches, and a study tool for going through a form one pose at a time. It is free, has no ads and needs no account.

  • Catalogue Forms library WT/Kukkiwon poomsae, ITF patterns and ATA Songahm forms, searchable by style, belt colour and difficulty.
  • Study lesson Taegeuk Il Jang An animated, step-through study aid with Korean terms, speed control and links to any pose.
  • Learn together Community Explore discussions about forms, technique and competitions, alongside practical learning resources.
