Jitae 지태

WT / Kukkiwon · 40 study poses · 28 reference counts

Open the full lesson Lesson answers All companions

Pose numbers count the lesson’s study poses; the count shows the movement or reference count the lesson labels. Left and right are the performer’s own. Each pose links to the lesson, paused on that pose.

Lesson: https://tkdflow.com/jitae.html

Poses

  1. Pose 1 Ready stance

    Ready position

    Stand ready, with your hands held in front.

  2. Pose 2 Left outside block

    Movement 1

    Set a back stance. Move your left forearm outward to block.

  3. Pose 3 Right high block

    Movement 2 (1)

    Set a front stance. Raise your right forearm above your head to block.

  4. Pose 4 Left middle punch

    Movement 2 (2)

    Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  5. Pose 5 Right outside block

    Movement 3 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a back stance. Move your right forearm outward to block.

  6. Pose 6 Left high block

    Movement 4 (1)

    Set a front stance. Raise your left forearm above your head to block.

  7. Pose 7 Right middle punch

    Movement 4 (2)

    Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  8. Pose 8 Left low block

    Movement 5 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a front stance. Sweep your left arm down for a low block.

  9. Pose 9 Left knifehand high block

    Movement 6

    Set a back stance. Raise your left knifehand above your head to block.

  10. Pose 10 Right front kick

    Movement 7 (1)

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a front kick.

  11. Pose 11 double knifehand low block

    Movement 7 (2)

    Land in a back stance. Lower the front knifehand while the other hand guards your body.

  12. Pose 12 Right outside block

    Movement 8

    Move your right forearm outward to block.

  13. Pose 13 Left front kick

    Movement 9 (1)

    Chamber your left knee, then extend a front kick.

  14. Pose 14 double knifehand low block

    Movement 9 (2)

    Land in a back stance. Lower the front knifehand while the other hand guards your body.

  15. Pose 15 Left high block

    Movement 10

    Set a front stance. Raise your left forearm above your head to block.

  16. Pose 16 small hinge

    Movement 11 (1)

    Set a front stance. Draw both fists back together beside your hip.

  17. Pose 17 diamond front punch

    Movement 11 (2)

    Raise one arm high while the other fist punches forward.

  18. Pose 18 Left inside block

    Movement 12 (1)

    Sweep your left forearm inward across your body.

  19. Pose 19 Right assisted inside block

    Movement 12 (2)

    Block inward with your right forearm, supported by the other hand.

  20. Pose 20 Left knifehand low block

    Movement 13

    Set a back stance. Sweep your left knifehand down to block.

  21. Pose 21 Right front kick

    Movement 14 (1)

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a front kick.

  22. Pose 22 Right middle punch

    Movement 14 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  23. Pose 23 Left middle punch

    Movement 14 (3)

    Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  24. Pose 24 bull block

    Movement 15 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a horse stance. Bring both forearms up to guard above your head.

  25. Pose 25 Left low side block

    Movement 16

    Sweep your left arm down and out for the low side block.

  26. Pose 26 Right knifehand side block

    Movement 17

    Extend your right knifehand out to the side.

  27. Pose 27 Left target hammerfist

    Movement 18 Kihap

    Strike down with your left hammerfist toward the opposite palm. Finish with a strong shout.

  28. Pose 28 Right low side block

    Movement 19

    Balance on your left leg. Sweep your right arm down and out for the low side block.

  29. Pose 29 small hinge

    Movement 20

    Balance on your left leg. Draw both fists back together beside your hip.

  30. Pose 30 Right side kick

    Movement 21 (1)

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a side kick.

  31. Pose 31 Left low side block

    Movement 21 (2)

    Balance on your right leg. Sweep your left arm down and out for the low side block.

  32. Pose 32 small hinge

    Movement 22

    Balance on your right leg. Draw both fists back together beside your hip.

  33. Pose 33 Left side kick

    Movement 23 (1)

    Chamber your left knee, then extend a side kick.

  34. Pose 34 Right middle punch

    Movement 23 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  35. Pose 35 Right middle punch

    Movement 24 Kihap

    Set a front stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist. Finish with a strong shout.

  36. Pose 36 double knifehand low block

    Movement 25 ↶ Turn left 270°

    Make a three-quarter turn to your left, then set a back stance. Lower the front knifehand while the other hand guards your body.

  37. Pose 37 double knifehand block

    Movement 26

    Set a back stance. Extend the front knifehand while the other hand guards your body.

  38. Pose 38 double knifehand low block

    Movement 27 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a back stance. Lower the front knifehand while the other hand guards your body.

  39. Pose 39 double knifehand block

    Movement 28

    Set a back stance. Extend the front knifehand while the other hand guards your body.

  40. Pose 40 Return to ready

    Return

    Return to the ready position.
