Pose numbers count the lesson’s study poses; the count shows the movement or reference count the lesson labels. Left and right are the performer’s own. Each pose links to the lesson, paused on that pose.

Poses

  1. Pose 1 Ready stance

    Ready position

    Stand with your feet together and hands in front of your abdomen.

  2. Pose 2 Left reverse knifehand block

    Movement 1 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a back stance. Block with the thumb side of your left hand.

  3. Pose 3 Left front kick

    Movement 2

    Chamber your left knee, then extend a low front kick.

  4. Pose 4 Right upward palm block

    Movement 3

    Land in a rear-foot stance. Lift your right palm upward to block.

  5. Pose 5 Right reverse knifehand block

    Movement 4 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a back stance. Block with the thumb side of your right hand.

  6. Pose 6 Right front kick

    Movement 5

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a low front kick.

  7. Pose 7 Left upward palm block

    Movement 6

    Land in a rear-foot stance. Lift your left palm upward to block.

  8. Pose 8 Left knifehand block

    Movement 7 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a back stance. Extend your left knifehand to block.

  9. Pose 9 Right upward elbow

    Movement 8

    Set a walking stance. Drive your right elbow upward.

  10. Pose 10 Right knifehand block

    Movement 9

    Set a back stance. Extend your right knifehand to block.

  11. Pose 11 Left upward elbow

    Movement 10

    Set a walking stance. Drive your left elbow upward.

  12. Pose 12 twin vertical punch

    Movement 11

    Set a walking stance. Punch forward with both fists held vertically.

  13. Pose 13 double uppercut

    Movement 12

    Set a walking stance. Drive both fists forward and upward together.

  14. Pose 14 x rising block

    Movement 13 ↶ Turn left 180°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Cross both forearms above your head in a rising block.

  15. Pose 15 Left backfist

    Movement 14 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a back stance. Strike forward with your left backfist.

  16. Pose 16 Left wrist release

    Movement 15

    Set a walking stance. Rotate and lift your left wrist to release the grip.

  17. Pose 17 Right high punch

    Movement 16

    Punch high with your right fist.

  18. Pose 18 Right backfist

    Movement 17 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a back stance. Strike forward with your right backfist.

  19. Pose 19 Right wrist release

    Movement 18

    Set a walking stance. Rotate and lift your right wrist to release the grip.

  20. Pose 20 Left high punch

    Movement 19

    Punch high with your left fist.

  21. Pose 21 Left assisted block

    Movement 20 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Block with your left forearm, supported by the other hand.

  22. Pose 22 Left middle punch

    Movement 21

    Set a back stance. Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  23. Pose 23 Right side kick

    Movement 22

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a side kick.

  24. Pose 24 Right assisted block

    Movement 23

    Land in a walking stance. Block with your right forearm, supported by the other hand.

  25. Pose 25 Right middle punch

  26. Pose 26 Left side kick

    Movement 25

    Movement 27

  29. Pose 29 Right forearm guarding block

    Movement 28

    Movement 29
