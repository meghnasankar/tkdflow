Dan-Gun 단군

ITF · 23 study poses · 21 reference counts

Open the full lesson Lesson answers All companions

Pose numbers count the lesson’s study poses; the count shows the movement or reference count the lesson labels. Left and right are the performer’s own. Each pose links to the lesson, paused on that pose.

Lesson: https://tkdflow.com/dan-gun.html

Poses

  1. Pose 1 Ready stance

    Ready position

    Stand ready, with your hands held in front.

  2. Pose 2 Left knifehand block

    Movement 1 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a back stance. Extend your left knifehand to block.

  3. Pose 3 Right high punch

    Movement 2

    Set a walking stance. Punch high with your right fist.

  4. Pose 4 Right knifehand block

    Movement 3 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a back stance. Extend your right knifehand to block.

  5. Pose 5 Left high punch

    Movement 4

    Set a walking stance. Punch high with your left fist.

  6. Pose 6 Left low block

    Movement 5 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Sweep your left arm down for a low block.

  7. Pose 7 Right high punch

    Movement 6

    Set a walking stance. Punch high with your right fist.

  8. Pose 8 Left high punch

    Movement 7

    Set a walking stance. Punch high with your left fist.

  9. Pose 9 Right high punch

    Movement 8

    Set a walking stance. Punch high with your right fist.

  10. Pose 10 Left twin forearm block

    Movement 9 ↶ Turn left 270°

    Make a three-quarter turn to your left, then set a back stance. Raise one forearm high while the other forearm blocks forward.

  11. Pose 11 Right high punch

    Movement 10

    Set a walking stance. Punch high with your right fist.

  12. Pose 12 Right twin forearm block

    Movement 11 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a back stance. Raise one forearm high while the other forearm blocks forward.

  13. Pose 13 Left high punch

    Movement 12

    Set a walking stance. Punch high with your left fist.

  14. Pose 14 Left low block

    Movement 13 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Sweep your left arm down for a low block.

  15. Pose 15 Left high block

    Movement 14

    Raise your left forearm above your head to block.

  16. Pose 16 Right high block

    Movement 15

    Set a walking stance. Raise your right forearm above your head to block.

  17. Pose 17 Left high block

    Movement 16

    Set a walking stance. Raise your left forearm above your head to block.

  18. Pose 18 Right high block

    Movement 17

    Set a walking stance. Raise your right forearm above your head to block.

  19. Pose 19 Left knifehand strike

    Movement 18 ↶ Turn left 270°

    Make a three-quarter turn to your left, then set a back stance. Strike across with the edge of your left hand.

  20. Pose 20 Right high punch

    Movement 19

    Set a walking stance. Punch high with your right fist.

  21. Pose 21 Right knifehand strike

    Movement 20 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a back stance. Strike across with the edge of your right hand.

  22. Pose 22 Left high punch

    Movement 21

    Set a walking stance. Punch high with your left fist.

  23. Pose 23 Return to ready

    Return

    Return to the ready position.
