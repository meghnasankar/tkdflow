Taegeuk Il Jang 태극 1장

WT / Kukkiwon · 20 study poses · 18 movements in the reference

Open the full lesson Lesson answers All companions

Pose numbers count the lesson’s study poses; the count shows the movement or reference count the lesson labels. Left and right are the performer’s own. Each pose links to the lesson, paused on that pose.

Lesson: https://tkdflow.com/taegeuk-1.html

Poses

  1. Pose 1 Junbi — Ready stance 준비

    Ready position

    Stand ready, with your hands held in front.

  2. Pose 2 Left Low Block 왼 아래막기

    Movement 1 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Sweep your left arm down to block.

  3. Pose 3 Right Middle Punch 오른 몸통 반대지르기

    Movement 2

    Step forward and punch to the middle with your right fist.

  4. Pose 4 Right Low Block 오른 아래막기

    Movement 3 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a walking stance. Sweep your right arm down to block.

  5. Pose 5 Left Middle Punch 왼 몸통 반대지르기

    Movement 4

    Step forward and punch to the middle with your left fist.

  6. Pose 6 Low Block + Middle Punch 왼 아래막기 · 오른 몸통 바로지르기

    Movements 5–6 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn into a front stance. Block low with your left arm, then punch right.

  7. Pose 7 Left Inside Block 왼 몸통 안막기

    Movement 7 ↷ Turn right 90°

    Turn right. Sweep your left forearm inward to block.

  8. Pose 8 Right Middle Punch 오른 몸통 바로지르기

    Movement 8

    Step forward and punch to the middle with your right fist.

  9. Pose 9 Right Inside Block 오른 몸통 안막기

    Movement 9 ↶ Turn left 180°

    Turn left. Sweep your right forearm inward to block.

  10. Pose 10 Left Middle Punch 왼 몸통 바로지르기

    Movement 10

    Step forward and punch to the middle with your left fist.

  11. Pose 11 Low Block + Middle Punch 오른 아래막기 · 왼 몸통 바로지르기

    Movements 11–12 ↷ Turn right 90°

    Turn into a front stance. Block low with your right arm, then punch left.

  12. Pose 12 Left High Block 왼 얼굴막기

    Movement 13 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left and raise your left forearm above your head to block.

  13. Pose 13 Right Front Kick 오른 앞차기

    Movement 14 (kick)

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a front kick.

  14. Pose 14 Right Middle Punch 오른 몸통 반대지르기

    Movement 14 (punch)

    Land with your right foot forward and punch with your right fist.

  15. Pose 15 Right High Block 오른 얼굴막기

    Movement 15 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right and raise your right forearm above your head to block.

  16. Pose 16 Left Front Kick 왼 앞차기

    Movement 16 (kick)

    Chamber your left knee, then extend a front kick.

  17. Pose 17 Left Middle Punch 왼 몸통 반대지르기

    Movement 16 (punch)

    Land with your left foot forward and punch with your left fist.

  18. Pose 18 Left Low Block 왼 아래막기

    Movement 17 ↷ Turn right 90°

    Turn into a front stance. Sweep your left arm down to block.

  19. Pose 19 Right Middle Punch 오른 몸통 반대지르기

    Movement 18 Kihap

    Step forward and punch with your right fist. Finish with a strong shout.

  20. Pose 20 Baro — Return to ready 바로 · 경례

    Return

    Return to the ready position.
