Songahm 3 송암 3

ATA / Songahm · 30 study poses · 28 reference counts

Open the full lesson Lesson answers All companions

Pose numbers count the lesson’s study poses; the count shows the movement or reference count the lesson labels. Left and right are the performer’s own. Each pose links to the lesson, paused on that pose.

Lesson: https://tkdflow.com/songahm-3.html

Poses

  1. Pose 1 Ready stance

    Ready position

    Stand ready, with your hands held in front.

  2. Pose 2 Left knifehand strike

    Movement 1

    Set a back stance. Strike across with the edge of your left hand.

  3. Pose 3 Left double knifehand block

    Movement 2

    Extend the front knifehand while the other hand guards your body.

  4. Pose 4 Right front kick

    Movement 3

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a front kick.

  5. Pose 5 Left roundhouse kick

    Movement 4

    Chamber your left knee, then extend a roundhouse kick.

  6. Pose 6 Left knifehand low block

    Movement 5

    Land in a front stance. Sweep your left knifehand down to block.

  7. Pose 7 Left knifehand high block

    Movement 6

    Raise your left knifehand above your head to block.

  8. Pose 8 Right middle punch

    Movement 7 Kihap

    Set a horse stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist. Finish with a strong shout.

  9. Pose 9 Left middle punch

    Movement 8 Kihap

    Punch to the middle with your left fist. Finish with a strong shout.

  10. Pose 10 Right spearhand

    Movement 9 ↶ Turn left 180°

    Turn left into a horse stance. Thrust your right fingertips forward with the other hand supporting.

  11. Pose 11 Left spearhand

    Movement 10

    Thrust your left fingertips forward with the other hand supporting.

  12. Pose 12 Right low block

    Movement 11

    Set a front stance. Sweep your right arm down for a low block.

  13. Pose 13 Left middle punch

    Movement 12

    Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  14. Pose 14 Right jump front kick

    Movement 13

    Lift off and extend a right front kick.

  15. Pose 15 Left middle punch

    Movement 14

    Land in a front stance. Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  16. Pose 16 Left low block

    Movement 15

    Set a front stance. Sweep your left arm down for a low block.

  17. Pose 17 Right middle punch

    Movement 16

    Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  18. Pose 18 Left jump front kick

    Movement 17 Kihap

    Lift off and extend a left front kick. Finish with a strong shout.

  19. Pose 19 Right middle punch

    Movement 18

    Land in a front stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  20. Pose 20 Right knifehand strike

    Movement 19

    Set a horse stance. Strike across with the edge of your right hand.

  21. Pose 21 Left backfist

    Movement 20

    Strike to the middle with your left backfist.

  22. Pose 22 Left knifehand strike

    Movement 21 ↶ Turn left 180°

    Turn left into a horse stance. Strike across with the edge of your left hand.

  23. Pose 23 Right backfist

    Movement 22

    Strike to the middle with your right backfist.

  24. Pose 24 Right knifehand strike

    Movement 23

    Set a back stance. Strike across with the edge of your right hand.

  25. Pose 25 Right double knifehand block

    Movement 24

    Extend the front knifehand while the other hand guards your body.

  26. Pose 26 Left front kick

    Movement 25

    Chamber your left knee, then extend a front kick.

  27. Pose 27 Right roundhouse kick

    Movement 26

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a roundhouse kick.

  28. Pose 28 Right knifehand low block

    Movement 27

    Land in a front stance. Sweep your right knifehand down to block.

  29. Pose 29 Right knifehand high block

    Movement 28

    Raise your right knifehand above your head to block.

  30. Pose 30 Return to ready

    Return

    Return to the ready position.
