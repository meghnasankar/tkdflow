Taebaek 태백

WT / Kukkiwon · 44 study poses · 26 reference counts

Open the full lesson Lesson answers All companions

Pose numbers count the lesson’s study poses; the count shows the movement or reference count the lesson labels. Left and right are the performer’s own. Each pose links to the lesson, paused on that pose.

Lesson: https://tkdflow.com/taebaek.html

Poses

  1. Pose 1 Ready stance

    Ready position

    Stand ready, with your hands held in front.

  2. Pose 2 knifehand low opening block

    Movement 1

    Set a cat stance. Spread both open hands outward into the low block.

  3. Pose 3 Right front kick

    Movement 2 (1)

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a front kick.

  4. Pose 4 Right middle punch

    Movement 2 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  5. Pose 5 Left middle punch

    Movement 2 (3)

    Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  6. Pose 6 knifehand low opening block

    Movement 3 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a cat stance. Spread both open hands outward into the low block.

  7. Pose 7 Left front kick

    Movement 4 (1)

    Chamber your left knee, then extend a front kick.

  8. Pose 8 Left middle punch

    Movement 4 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  9. Pose 9 Right middle punch

    Movement 4 (3)

    Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  10. Pose 10 Left knifehand high block + Right knifehand strike

    Movement 5 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a front stance. Raise your left knifehand above your head to block. Strike across with the edge of your right hand.

  11. Pose 11 Right palm block

    Movement 6 (1)

    Set a front stance. Press the attack away with your right palm.

  12. Pose 12 Left middle punch

    Movement 6 (2)

    Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  13. Pose 13 Left palm block

    Movement 7 (1)

    Set a front stance. Press the attack away with your left palm.

  14. Pose 14 Right middle punch

    Movement 7 (2)

    Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  15. Pose 15 Right palm block

    Movement 8 (1)

    Set a front stance. Press the attack away with your right palm.

  16. Pose 16 Left middle punch

    Movement 8 (2) Kihap

    Punch to the middle with your left fist. Finish with a strong shout.

  17. Pose 17 Left diamond middle block

    Movement 9 ↶ Turn left 270°

    Make a three-quarter turn to your left, then set a back stance. Set one forearm high and the other forward at the middle.

  18. Pose 18 Right pulling uppercut

    Movement 10

    Pull with one hand as your right fist rises in an uppercut.

  19. Pose 19 Left side punch

    Movement 11

    Punch out to the side with your left fist.

  20. Pose 20 small hinge

    Movement 12

    Balance on your right leg. Draw both fists back together beside your hip.

  21. Pose 21 Left side kick + Left side punch

    Movement 13 (1)

    Chamber your left knee, then extend a side kick. Punch out to the side with your left fist.

  22. Pose 22 Right elbow strike

    Movement 13 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Drive your right elbow toward the opposite open palm.

  23. Pose 23 small hinge

    Movement 14 (1) ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a closed stance. Draw both fists back together beside your hip.

  24. Pose 24 Right diamond middle block

    Movement 14 (2)

    Set one forearm high and the other forward at the middle.

  25. Pose 25 Left pulling uppercut

    Movement 15

    Pull with one hand as your left fist rises in an uppercut.

  26. Pose 26 Right side punch

    Movement 16

    Punch out to the side with your right fist.

  27. Pose 27 small hinge

    Movement 17

    Balance on your left leg. Draw both fists back together beside your hip.

  28. Pose 28 Right side kick + Right side punch

    Movement 18 (1)

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a side kick. Punch out to the side with your right fist.

  29. Pose 29 Left elbow strike

    Movement 18 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Drive your left elbow toward the opposite open palm.

  30. Pose 30 small hinge

    Movement 19 (1) ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a closed stance. Draw both fists back together beside your hip.

  31. Pose 31 double knifehand block

    Movement 19 (2)

    Extend the front knifehand while the other hand guards your body.

  32. Pose 32 Left palm block + Right spearhand

    Movement 20

    Set a front stance. Press downward with your left palm. Thrust your right fingertips forward as the other hand blocks.

  33. Pose 33 Right wrist release

    Movement 21 (1) ↶ Turn left 360°

    Turn all the way around to the left. Rotate and lift your right wrist to release the grip.

  34. Pose 34 Left backfist

    Movement 21 (2)

    Strike forward with your left backfist.

  35. Pose 35 Right middle punch

    Movement 22 Kihap

    Set a front stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist. Finish with a strong shout.

  36. Pose 36 Left scissor block

    Movement 23 ↶ Turn left 270°

    Make a three-quarter turn to your left, then set a front stance. Block outward with your left arm as the other arm blocks low.

  37. Pose 37 Right front kick

    Movement 24 (1)

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a front kick.

  38. Pose 38 Right middle punch

    Movement 24 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  39. Pose 39 Left middle punch

    Movement 24 (3)

    Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  40. Pose 40 Right scissor block

    Movement 25 ↷ Turn right 180°

  41. Pose 41 Left front kick

  42. Pose 42 Left middle punch
