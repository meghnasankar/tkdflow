Open the full lesson Lesson answers All companions

Pose numbers count the lesson’s study poses; the count shows the movement or reference count the lesson labels. Left and right are the performer’s own. Each pose links to the lesson, paused on that pose.

Lesson: https://tkdflow.com/sipjin.html

Poses

  1. Pose 1 Ready stance

    Ready position

    Stand ready, with your hands held in front.

  2. Pose 2 bull block

    Movement 1

    Bring both forearms up to guard above your head.

  3. Pose 3 Left palm assisted outside block

    Movement 2

    Set a back stance. Block outward with your left forearm and support it with your palm.

  4. Pose 4 Left wrist turn

    Movement 3 (1)

    Set a front stance. Turn your left wrist to change the hand position.

  5. Pose 5 Right spearhand

    Movement 3 (2)

    Thrust your right fingertips forward with the other hand supporting.

  6. Pose 6 Left middle punch

    Movement 3 (3)

    Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  7. Pose 7 Right middle punch

    Movement 3 (4)

    Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  8. Pose 8 opening mountain block

    Movement 4

    Set a horse stance. Open both arms outward into the mountain block.

  9. Pose 9 Left target grab

    Movement 5 (1)

    Set a cross stance. Reach out with your left hand to prepare the next strike.

  10. Pose 10 Right side punch

    Movement 5 (2) Kihap

    Punch out to the side with your right fist. Finish with a strong shout.

    Movement 6 ↶ Turn left 180°
