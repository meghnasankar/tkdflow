Taegeuk Yi Jang 태극 2장

WT / Kukkiwon · 25 study poses · 18 reference counts

Open the full lesson Lesson answers All companions

Pose numbers count the lesson’s study poses; the count shows the movement or reference count the lesson labels. Left and right are the performer’s own. Each pose links to the lesson, paused on that pose.

Lesson: https://tkdflow.com/taegeuk-2.html

Poses

  1. Pose 1 Ready stance

    Ready position

    Stand ready, with your hands held in front.

  2. Pose 2 Left low block

    Movement 1 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Sweep your left arm down for a low block.

  3. Pose 3 Right middle punch

    Movement 2

    Set a front stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  4. Pose 4 Right low block

    Movement 3 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a walking stance. Sweep your right arm down for a low block.

  5. Pose 5 Left middle punch

    Movement 4

    Set a front stance. Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  6. Pose 6 Right inside block

    Movement 5 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Sweep your right forearm inward across your body.

  7. Pose 7 Left inside block

    Movement 6

    Set a walking stance. Sweep your left forearm inward across your body.

  8. Pose 8 Left low block

    Movement 7 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Sweep your left arm down for a low block.

  9. Pose 9 Right front kick

    Movement 8 (1)

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a front kick.

  10. Pose 10 Right high punch

    Movement 8 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Punch high with your right fist.

  11. Pose 11 Right low block

    Movement 9 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a walking stance. Sweep your right arm down for a low block.

  12. Pose 12 Left front kick

    Movement 10 (1)

    Chamber your left knee, then extend a front kick.

  13. Pose 13 Left high punch

    Movement 10 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Punch high with your left fist.

  14. Pose 14 Left high block

    Movement 11 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Raise your left forearm above your head to block.

  15. Pose 15 Right high block

    Movement 12

    Set a walking stance. Raise your right forearm above your head to block.

  16. Pose 16 Right inside block

    Movement 13 ↶ Turn left 270°

    Make a three-quarter turn to your left, then set a walking stance. Sweep your right forearm inward across your body.

  17. Pose 17 Left inside block

    Movement 14 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a walking stance. Sweep your left forearm inward across your body.

  18. Pose 18 Left low block

    Movement 15 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Sweep your left arm down for a low block.

  19. Pose 19 Right front kick

    Movement 16 (1)

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a front kick.

  20. Pose 20 Right middle punch

    Movement 16 (2)

    Land in a walking stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  21. Pose 21 Left front kick

    Movement 17 (1)

    Chamber your left knee, then extend a front kick.

  22. Pose 22 Left middle punch

    Movement 17 (2)

    Land in a walking stance. Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  23. Pose 23 Right front kick

    Movement 18 (1)

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a front kick.

  24. Pose 24 Right middle punch

    Movement 18 (2) Kihap

    Land in a walking stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist. Finish with a strong shout.

  25. Pose 25 Return to ready

    Return

    Return to the ready position.
