Taegeuk Sam Jang 태극 3장

WT / Kukkiwon · 36 study poses · 20 reference counts

Open the full lesson Lesson answers All companions

Pose numbers count the lesson’s study poses; the count shows the movement or reference count the lesson labels. Left and right are the performer’s own. Each pose links to the lesson, paused on that pose.

Lesson: https://tkdflow.com/taegeuk-3.html

Poses

  1. Pose 1 Ready stance

    Ready position

    Stand ready, with your hands held in front.

  2. Pose 2 Left low block

    Movement 1 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Sweep your left arm down for a low block.

  3. Pose 3 Right front kick

    Movement 2 (1)

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a front kick.

  4. Pose 4 Right middle punch

    Movement 2 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  5. Pose 5 Left middle punch

    Movement 2 (3)

    Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  6. Pose 6 Right low block

    Movement 3 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a walking stance. Sweep your right arm down for a low block.

  7. Pose 7 Left front kick

    Movement 4 (1)

    Chamber your left knee, then extend a front kick.

  8. Pose 8 Left middle punch

    Movement 4 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  9. Pose 9 Right middle punch

    Movement 4 (3)

    Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  10. Pose 10 Right knifehand strike

    Movement 5 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Strike across with the edge of your right hand.

  11. Pose 11 Left knifehand strike

    Movement 6

    Set a walking stance. Strike across with the edge of your left hand.

  12. Pose 12 Left knifehand block

    Movement 7 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a back stance. Extend your left knifehand to block.

  13. Pose 13 Right middle punch

    Movement 8

    Set a front stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  14. Pose 14 Right knifehand block

    Movement 9 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a back stance. Extend your right knifehand to block.

  15. Pose 15 Left middle punch

    Movement 10

    Set a front stance. Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  16. Pose 16 Right inside block

    Movement 11 ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Sweep your right forearm inward across your body.

  17. Pose 17 Left inside block

    Movement 12

    Set a walking stance. Sweep your left forearm inward across your body.

  18. Pose 18 Left low block

    Movement 13 ↶ Turn left 270°

    Make a three-quarter turn to your left, then set a walking stance. Sweep your left arm down for a low block.

  19. Pose 19 Right front kick

    Movement 14 (1)

    Chamber your right knee, then extend a front kick.

  20. Pose 20 Right middle punch

    Movement 14 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  21. Pose 21 Left middle punch

    Movement 14 (3)

    Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  22. Pose 22 Right low block

    Movement 15 ↷ Turn right 180°

    Turn right into a walking stance. Sweep your right arm down for a low block.

  23. Pose 23 Left front kick

    Movement 16 (1)

    Chamber your left knee, then extend a front kick.

  24. Pose 24 Left middle punch

    Movement 16 (2)

    Land in a front stance. Punch to the middle with your left fist.

  25. Pose 25 Right middle punch

    Movement 16 (3)

    Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  26. Pose 26 Left low block

    Movement 17 (1) ↶ Turn left 90°

    Turn left into a walking stance. Sweep your left arm down for a low block.

  27. Pose 27 Right middle punch

    Movement 17 (2)

    Punch to the middle with your right fist.

  28. Pose 28 Right low block

    Movement 18 (1)

  29. Pose 29 Left middle punch

    Movement 18 (2)
