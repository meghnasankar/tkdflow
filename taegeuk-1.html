Taegeuk Il Jang 태극 1장

The first of the eight Taegeuk forms, built on the trigram ☰ Geon (heaven and light). It is commonly counted as 18 movements — a draft mapping until an instructor reviews it. The animation shows 20 poses: it adds the ready and return positions and splits each front kick from the punch that follows it.

Jump to the player

Not yet reviewed by a qualified instructor. Use this as a study aid alongside your own instructor’s teaching. The movement numbers, Korean terms and kihap points below are a draft.
