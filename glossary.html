Taekwondo words explained

Short, plain-English meanings for common Korean taekwondo terms, with lesson examples where the forms have them.

These terms and spellings come from Kukkiwon’s Taekwondo Technical Terminology (English edition, 2011). Some, such as Gyeorugi and Gyeokpa, aren’t part of these form lessons. Other styles and schools may use different names for similar techniques.
