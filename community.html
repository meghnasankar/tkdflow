Community guidelines

Be respectful, keep questions specific, credit references and post only material you have permission to share. Do not post contact details, school schedules, spam or harmful content. Public submissions are moderated. Public reading is open to everyone; accounts aged 13–17 remain private and do not publish posts or messages.

Question about a lesson pose? Read public lesson answers. Members aged 13 and over with a confirmed account and completed profile can also ask TKDflow community moderators a private question from the “Help with this lesson” section of any lesson. Private questions are not published; public answers are written separately. How saving and lesson help work
