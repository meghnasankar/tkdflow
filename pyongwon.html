Pyongwon 평원

Represents the vast plain and the dominance and grace found in open space. Study the source-based sequence with the full-body 3D character, a camera you can turn to any side, slow playback and frame controls. Combination counts are split into separate study poses.

Keys (with the player selected): ← → step one frame (Shift for a pose), Home End first/last pose, Space or K play/pause, R replay the current move once. Camera: A D move around (Shift for the next side), W S raise or lower, + - zoom, 0 reset, 1 2 3 whole body, armwork or footwork.
