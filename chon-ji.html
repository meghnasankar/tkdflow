ITF Beginner 19 reference counts

Chon-Ji 천지

The very first ITF pattern, meaning Heaven and Earth. Contains 19 movements and is the gateway to the ITF curriculum. Study the source-based sequence with the full-body 3D character, fixed camera views, slow playback and frame controls. Combination counts are split into separate study poses.
